# Kodak Gold 200 Grain v2 bank

Film-Juicer generated the record and summed-area-table payloads from its
[spektrafilm](https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm)-derived Kodak Gold
200 profile by Andrea Volpato, distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The bank uses
Film-Juicer's evaluated CDF3 response at gamma 1 with DIR off.

The [Fotufilm engine](https://fotufilm.com), copyright 2026 MUAStudio Inc.,
generated the coating at revision
[`87a3f46af093a9e6bf76acf21aca83d3b9e2c600`](https://github.com/DhaliwalX/fotufilm-engine/tree/87a3f46af093a9e6bf76acf21aca83d3b9e2c600).

Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license.
[CC BY-SA 4.0 license text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode).

The version 1 manifest identifies the stock and its payload SHA-256 digests.
The strength of 0.0105 at the 48 µm reference aperture is an authored Film-Juicer
research estimate. The bank uses the G2 calibration in
[`tests/grain/calibration/gold-200.json`](../../../tests/grain/calibration/gold-200.json).
The bundle installs this notice as `Contents/Legal/fotufilm/GOLD-200-NOTICE.md`
with the engine license and the spektrafilm attribution and license.
